Ganfan recipe

Description

Ganfan is a dish eaten in Central-Asia originated mainly form uyghurs. I found it very close to my own taste. I hope you will like it

Ingridients

  • rice
  • beef or lamb
  • balgarian pepper
  • onion
  • oil
  • tomatoes
  • potatoes
  • garlic
  • tomato paste
  • pekin cabbage
  • salt

Instructions

  1. preapare the rice wash the rice 3 times in cold water."150~200g of raw rice for one pesron"
  2. to boil the rice add the proportions of rice in grams and water equal. ex: 200 gram of rice add 200 ml of water and boil it until the water evoporates
  3. Wash all the vegetables and cut the onions into thin slices, cabbage potatoes and balgarian pepper cut into thin rectangles, and cut cut tomatoes into cubes
  4. Slice the meat into thin rectangles and then heat up the frying pan
  5. Stir fry the meat and onions then add potatoes and fry for 3 minute more
  6. Then add pepper,cabbage ,garlic, salt and tomatoes and fry and mix them
  7. Add 5 tbl spoons fo tomato paste mix everything. And then add a water soo that everything will be a liitle bit under the water and close it with a lid and led it boil for ~ 20 minutes in low-midium heat
  8. lastly put the rice into the dish and add the soup on top and enjoy your meal
